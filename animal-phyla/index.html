The 9 Major Animal Phyla
Phylum Meaning Key Feature Example ~Species
Porifera "Pore bearer" Filter feeding, no true tissues Sponge ~5,000
Cnidaria "Stinging nettle" Cnidocytes (stinging cells) Jellyfish ~11,000
Platyhelminthes "Flat worms" Flat body, no body cavity Planarian ~25,000
Nematoda "Thread-like" Round body, pseudocoelom Roundworm ~25,000+
Annelida "Little ring" Segmented body Earthworm ~22,000
Arthropoda "Jointed foot" Exoskeleton, jointed legs Beetle ~1,100,000
Mollusca "Soft" Mantle, muscular foot Snail ~85,000
Echinodermata "Spiny skin" 5-fold symmetry, water vascular Sea star ~7,000
Chordata "Cord" Notochord/backbone Frog ~100,000
Standards Covered
SOL LS.7

The student will investigate and understand that organisms can be classified based on shared characteristics. Key ideas include the distinguishing characteristics of kingdoms; the major animal and plant phyla; and the use of classification systems.
